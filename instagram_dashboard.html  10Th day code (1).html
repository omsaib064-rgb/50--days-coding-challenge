Stories Lead Engagement
Stories achieve the highest avg engagement rate (16.03%), narrowly ahead of Reels (15.43%). Despite Videos reaching the most users on average (26,405), their ER lags at only 9.26%.
02
📅
November Peaked, April Reached
November had the highest avg ER (21.59%) while April drove the most reach (32,631 avg) — suggesting different months serve different goals: engagement vs visibility.
03
📝
Medium Captions Win
Posts with 31–100 word captions scored highest (15.01% ER). Very short captions underperformed at 9.91%, suggesting that moderate storytelling in captions resonates best with audiences.
04
#️⃣
More Hashtags = More Engagement
Posts with 21+ hashtags average 15.26% ER vs 13.66% for low hashtag posts. However, the difference is modest — hashtag count alone is not the primary driver.
05
👥
Smaller Accounts Punch Higher
Accounts with fewer followers consistently show higher engagement rates. The top 5 posts all came from accounts with under 15,000 followers — micro-accounts drive disproportionate engagement.
06
🏆
Reel P1049 is Champion
The top post (Reel P1049, Nov 2024, ER = 140.79%) generated 7,341 likes from just 5,526 followers. This outlier illustrates how viral Reels can dramatically exceed expected performance.
