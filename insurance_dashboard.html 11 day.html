Key Findings & Recommendations

📋 Executive Summary

🚨 Fraud Hotspot: Major Damage

A striking 60.5% fraud rate among "Major Damage" claims vs. only 10–13% for Minor/Total Loss. Suggests fraudsters deliberately inflate damage severity. Recommend mandatory independent assessor for all Major Damage claims.

📍 Geographic Concentration

NY and SC together account for 52% of all claims and $28.3M in payouts. NY leads with 262 claims ($14.8M). Regional task forces and stricter underwriting in these states could yield significant savings.

🚗 Vehicle Claims Dominate Payouts

Vehicle damage constitutes 72% of avg total claim ($37,929 of $52,762). Injury and property claims each contribute ~14%. Partnering with certified repair shops and introducing vehicle valuation caps is advised.

⏰ High-Risk Hours: 10AM, 2–4PM, 6PM

Fraud incidents cluster at hours 10, 14–16, and 23. These windows show disproportionate fraud-to-legitimate ratios. Automated time-anomaly flags during claim intake can catch suspicious patterns early.

👔 Occupation Risk Pattern

Exec-managerial, craft-repair, and machine-op roles account for the most fraud claims. Targeted fraud scoring models for these professions during new policy binding could reduce exposure.

📊 Loss Ratio Warning

Total annual premium collected ($1.26M) vs. total claims paid ($52.8M) represents a loss ratio of 4,200%+ — indicating premiums are critically underpriced relative to claims. Immediate actuarial review is recommended.
