05 · Business Insights
🔥
Tables Are a Profit Black Hole
Tables generate $207K in sales yet lose $17,725. With an average discount of 26.1% — the highest in the catalog — every Tables order statistically burns money. The product exists to grow sales, not profit.
⚠️
Discounts Beyond 30% Kill All Profit
Orders with 31–50% discounts lose $48K cumulatively. Orders above 50% lose $76.5K. The discount-profit correlation is −0.22, confirming that aggressive discounting is systematically eroding margins.
📍
Central Region — The Weak Link
Central earns only $39.6K profit on $500K sales — a 7.9% margin compared to West's 14.9%. The region's over-reliance on discounted Furniture may be the primary cause of underperformance.
🏆
Copiers & Technology Are Stars
Copiers deliver a 37.2% margin — the best in the business. Technology as a whole earns $145K profit on $836K sales. Expanding Technology SKUs with controlled discounts is the fastest path to margin recovery.
🗺️
Texas & Ohio Drag the Numbers
Texas loses $25.7K and Ohio $16.9K. Together, these two states account for over $42K in losses. Targeted pricing audits in these states could recover significant bottom-line value.
📦
Furniture Margin is Near-Zero
Furniture generates $741K in revenue but only $18.4K profit — a paper-thin 2.49% margin. Three sub-categories (Tables, Bookcases, Supplies) actively lose money, dragging the entire category into near-breakeven territory.
06 · Business Story — Why Profits Dropped
01
Sales Rose. Profits Didn't Follow.
The company achieved $2.3M in total sales — a volume metric leadership celebrated. But beneath the revenue headline, only $286K in profit remained — a 12.4% net margin that sounds acceptable until you look deeper. Of 9,977 orders, 1,869 (18.7%) lost money. For nearly 1 in 5 transactions, the company was paying to serve its customers.
02
The Discount Trap
To maintain sales momentum, the team leaned on discounting. But the data reveals a brutal truth: discounts above 30% guarantee cumulative losses. The 51%+ discount bucket alone destroyed $76,553 in profit. Discounting was used as a volume lever — but it was actually a profit accelerant in reverse.
03
Furniture: The Category Anchor
Furniture is the company's second-largest revenue category at $741K — yet it contributes just $18.4K in profit. Tables alone lose $17,725. The category is a strategic dead weight: it consumes warehouse space, logistics cost, and operational attention while delivering almost zero financial return.
04
Geography Compounds the Problem
The Central region's 7.9% margin is less than half that of the West. States like Texas (−$25.7K), Ohio (−$16.9K), and Pennsylvania (−$15.5K) are not just underperforming — they are actively destroying national profitability. No regional pricing or discount strategy appears to be in place.
07 · Strategic Recommendations
REC · 01
Cap Discounts at 25%
Implement a hard discount ceiling of 25% across all categories. Require VP-level approval for any exception. Model shows this alone could recover $76K+ in annual profit from the 51%+ bucket.
REC · 02
Discontinue or Reprice Tables
Tables lose $17,725 per year. Either reprice them 15–20% higher or discontinue the lowest-performing SKUs. A cost audit may reveal supplier pricing needs renegotiation.
REC · 03
Invest in Technology Category
Technology earns 17.4% margin — nearly 7× Furniture. Shift marketing budget toward Copiers, Phones, and Accessories. These categories deliver profit at scale without heavy discounting.
REC · 04
Regional Pricing Strategy
Introduce region-specific minimum price floors in Texas, Ohio, and Pennsylvania. These three states collectively lose $58.2K. Targeted margin recovery here is faster than growing sales.
REC · 05
Quarterly Discount Audit
Establish a quarterly review of discount levels by sub-category and region. Flag any sub-category where avg discount exceeds 20% and margin falls below 10%. Automate alerts in BI dashboards.
REC · 06
Bookcases & Supplies Review
Both sub-categories are loss-making. Evaluate supplier contracts and shipping costs. Consider bundling them with high-margin Office Supplies items to lift basket profitability.
